AURAFLOW 2.7.1 — ONE HANDS-FREE WORKSPACE

1. Close the old app and extract this ZIP into a new folder.
2. Use 64-bit Python 3.11 or 3.12 (3.13 remains unsupported by pinned dependencies).
3. Double-click Start_AuraFlow.bat. Camera opens automatically.
4. Click My hands. Enroll each hand separately with an open palm for about 4 seconds.
5. Close enrollment. Show an open palm briefly to verify, then use any gesture.
   Keep your hand in view. If tracking is lost, show the open palm again.
   Existing 2.7 profiles still work; you do not need to enroll again.

HAND FILTER: Enabled by default. Enroll both hands for all controls. All visible
hands must verify first; palm tracking then allows fingers to curl freely.
Uncertain tracking blocks controls. Profiles stay local across launches.
My hands can re-enroll or forget profiles. Settings can change sensitivity or disable
the filter. This is approximate geometry matching: similar hands can still pass,
and your own hand may sometimes be rejected. It is not secure identity recognition.

TWO-HAND ZOOM: pinch thumb/index on BOTH hands, then spread apart or bring together.
TWO FISTS: reset zoom. TWO V SIGNS: screenshot. TWO PALMS: show desktop.
LEFT THUMBS-UP ALONE: hold 0.9 seconds to pause/resume. Briefly close your visible hands after a hold action.

Right pinky+pinch = volume; left = brightness.
Right point/pinch = pointer/click. V sign = scroll.
Left fist = play/pause; right fist = mute.
Open-palm swipe = tracks; three-finger swipe = browser tabs.

Settings are mouse-controlled. If labels are still reversed, enable Swap anatomical
left/right labels. Windows must allow camera access; the app does not ask permission.
See README.md for the full guide and compatibility notes.

TABS: Raise index+middle+ring. Swipe right for next tab or left for previous,
as seen in the preview. Make a loose fist for 0.25 seconds, then open your hand to repeat. Keep the
browser focused. The full gesture guide is now under View all gestures.

DESKTOP APPS: four fingers up, thumb tucked across palm; swipe right for Alt+Tab
or left for Alt+Shift+Tab. Make a loose fist for 0.25 seconds, then open your hand to repeat.
Three fingers = browser tabs. All five fingers open = music tracks.

Right-hand pinch clicks cannot activate the pause gesture. Only left thumbs-up
with no second hand visible can pause or resume.

KEEP YOUR HAND IN VIEW: Swipe, briefly make a loose fist, then open for the next
gesture. The reset fist does not mute or play/pause. No need to leave the frame.

MINI CAMERA: click Mini camera on the dashboard for a small always-on-top live
preview. Gesture controls keep running. Click Dashboard or close it to return.

CLOSE APP: focus the desired app, raise ONLY your middle finger, tuck thumb and
other fingers, and hold one hand steady for 1.2 seconds. Sends Alt+F4 once.
Save prompts remain for you to handle. Brief fist, then reopen to reset in view.
Use the Close app demo to practice safely. No close gesture runs while paused.
